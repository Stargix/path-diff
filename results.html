Reading the metrics

Original task pass combines arrival, pose, timing and a route-average height criterion. Height scoring excludes ±0.25 m around posture changes and accepts MAE up to 4 cm. This can accept an omitted intermediate posture; it should not be read as completing every change.

The all-section check on the main page is a separate, retrospective diagnostic: every requested section must have spatial height MAE ≤4 cm, including its transition response. It does not require instantaneous height attainment and is not a smoothness measure. A short response delay can fail this stricter check even when the target height is eventually reached.

Section errors use first-passage samples on a 1 cm spatial grid. The first 4 cm and final 15 cm are outside its coverage domain; one missing initial grid cell is tolerated for post-step logging. Missing internal or terminal coverage fails the section check. No missing height is imputed. These conventions are exploratory, not independently validated task tolerances.

Active CTE is temporal lateral RMSE. Active height MAE is temporal absolute height error. The main tables report their means over arrived episodes, then over trained actors; the counts are shown. They are not the same as spatial section errors. Full active CTE retains the method-dependent drift during initialization.

Variation of normalized action commands and body tilt remain available in the underlying evidence, but are not used as a physical-smoothness leaderboard: their eligible samples differ, and neither is physical jerk. Establishing smoother motion requires synchronized motion measurements and response-duration controls.

Green on the main page identifies the largest displayed pass-rate mean within a defined block, including ties. It does not indicate statistical significance. Conditional tracking errors and unequal speed extrapolation tests are not assigned a green winner.
